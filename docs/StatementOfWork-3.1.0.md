### `6f6ed4dd7` — Forward-port 3.0.1 SAML hotfix stack to develop (#906)

_**Deferred:**_

> Settings notice when /patrons/me/ has been 401-ing for
> >24h to surface annotation-sync degradation to power users. Not urgent;
> bearer-served features keep working transparently.
> 
> Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 4.7 (1M context) <noreply@anthropic.com>
> 
> * Forward-port: TPPBookRegistry wait-for-loaded + MyBooks/Holds authState observer
> 
> Completes the 3.0.1 hotfix forward-port. The original 906 commit
> (61c740a20) covered the responder/modal/half-sheet/audiobook fixes but
> omitted two safety nets the hotfix needed in production:
> 
> (1) **TPPBookRegistry.sync wait-for-loaded wrapper** (mirror of hotfix
>     a25d23c54). `BookRegistrySync.sync` deliberately bails when state
>     is `.unloaded`/`.loading` to protect on-disk records from feed-only
>     reconciliation. Post-sign-in callers, however, legitimately want a
>     sync — and on SAML re-auth the registry can land in `.unloaded`
>     between sign-out and sign-in, silently skipping the sync. Wraps
>     `TPPBookRegistry.sync(completion:)` to subscribe to
>     `TPPBookRegistryStateDidChange` and run sync once state reaches
>     `.loaded`/`.synced`, then kicks off `load()` (no-op if already in
>     flight). Handles both the simple `.unloaded` case and the in-flight
>     `.loading` race where `loadingAccount` sticks after sign-out
>     interrupts a load mid-flight. One-shot observer self-removes.
> 
> (2) **MyBooks/Holds authStateDidChangePublisher observer with
>     hasCredentials guard** (mirror of hotfix d1e216805 + df4cf7938).
>     Subscribes to `UserAccountPublisher.shared.authStateDidChangePublisher`
>     on both view models, fires `reloadData()`/`refresh()` on `.loggedIn`
>     transition. Catches both fresh sign-in (`.loggedOut → .loggedIn`)
>     and SAML re-auth (`.credentialsStale → .loggedIn`) — the latter is
>     invisible to `hasCredentials`-based publishers because the keychain
>     stays populated through stale state.
> 
>     The hasCredentials guard inside the sink is for test isolation:
>     `UserAccountPublisher.shared` is singleton-backed and authState
>     leaks across XCTest cases. Without the guard, the observer fires
>     in tests against a mock account that has no real credentials and
>     clobbers test fixtures (18 MyBooks tests regressed without it on
>     hotfix; df4cf7938 adds it).
> 
> Verified: full build green on develop tip with both ports applied.
> 
> The CI keychain-skip on AccountDetailCredentialStateTests (4th hotfix
> piece) was already merged to develop separately — no port needed there.
